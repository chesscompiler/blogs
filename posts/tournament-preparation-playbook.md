Tournament success starts before round one. Preparation should reduce decision fatigue and preserve focus.

## 7-Day Pre-Tournament Plan
- Days 7–5: opening refresh + tactical sharpness
- Days 4–3: endgame technique + model games
- Days 2–1: light review, sleep priority, no heavy new theory

## Round-Day Checklist
- 10-minute tactical warm-up
- Hydration + stable meal timing
- Opening file review: only your first 8–10 moves
- Post-game notes immediately after result

Process consistency outperforms pre-round panic prep.
