Chess strength is not only knowledge—it is decision quality under stress.

## Common Mental Leaks
- Moving too fast after a blunder
- Over-optimism in winning positions
- Fear-based passivity when worse

## In-Game Reset Protocol (60 Seconds)
1. Sit back, unclench shoulders
2. Breathe slowly for 3 cycles
3. Recalculate forcing moves only
4. Pick safest strong option

## Post-Loss Recovery
- Write one lesson, one strength, one next action
- Avoid blitz revenge sessions immediately
- Sleep and review next day with objectivity

Mental routines make your technical strength available when it matters most.
