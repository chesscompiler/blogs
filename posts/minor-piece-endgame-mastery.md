Minor-piece endings reward understanding more than memorization. Your plan should follow pawn structure and square color control.

## Knight Endgame Guidelines
- Knights love fixed pawn chains
- Centralized king amplifies knight forks
- Outside passed pawn can distract king

## Bishop Endgame Guidelines
- Bishop shines with pawns on opposite color
- Long diagonals + two weaknesses win games
- Opposite-color bishops often draw, unless kings are exposed

The bishop side often improves by creating a second target.

## Conversion Habit
In simplified positions, think in plans of 5–8 moves, not single tactics. Endgames punish impatience more than calculation errors.
