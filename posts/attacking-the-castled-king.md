A successful kingside attack is rarely a random pawn storm. It’s usually built on development lead, central control, and piece coordination.

## Conditions for a Sound Attack
- Your king is safer than opponent king
- You have more active pieces near the target
- Center is stable or can be closed in your favor

White prepares pressure before committing to direct sacrifices.

## Attack Blueprint
1. Bring queen + rook to attacking files.
2. Reposition knight to f5/e6 outposts.
3. Create one concrete threat, then increase pressure.

## When to Stop
If opponent can force queen trade or open your own king, switch to positional conversion. Strong attackers know when to transition, not just when to push.
