Chess isn't just about the games you win; it's about the games you *don't lose*. Every grandmaster is a world-class defender. When your back is against the wall, you need a cool head and a specific set of tools.

## 🧱 1. Create a Fortress

Sometimes, even if you are down material, you can set up a position where the opponent simply cannot break through.

<chess-board fen="8/8/8/4k3/8/5p2/5P2/4K3 w - - 0 1" highlight="f2,p3,e1" arrows="e1-d1,d1-e1"></chess-board>
*A classic pawn fortress. As long as White keeps the King on e1/d1, Black cannot promote.*

---

## 🎭 2. The Art of the Stalemate Trick

When all else fails, look for a stalemate. This is especially common in rook endgames or when your king is trapped in a corner.

<chess-board fen="7k/5Q2/8/8/8/8/8/7K b - - 0 1"></chess-board>
*The "Desperado" Rook: If you can sacrifice your last pieces so that you have no legal moves left, you've saved half a point!*

---

## ⚡ 3. Counter-Attack (The Best Defense)

Active defense is usually better than passive defense. If your opponent is attacking your king, can you create threats against *their* king? Or perhaps attack their unprotected pieces?

**Ask yourself:** "If I lose anyway, what's the most annoying thing I can do to my opponent right now?"

---

## 🧠 The Defensive Mindset

- **Don't Panic:** Most "winning" attacks have a flaw. If you stay calm, you might find the one move that holds the position.
- **Make them earn it:** Don't resign just because you're down a pawn. Force your opponent to show they know how to convert.
- **Identify the "Only Move":** In many defensive positions, there is only one move that doesn't lose immediately. Find it, play it, and survive another turn.

> 💡 **Tip:** Practice "Defense" puzzles. Most puzzles ask you to find a win, but training yourself to find the saving draw is just as valuable for your rating.

**Next time you're in trouble, don't give up. Channel your inner Tigran Petrosian and build a wall!**
