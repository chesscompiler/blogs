Defense is not passive suffering; it is active resource-finding. Many half-points come from accurate defensive technique.

## Defensive Priorities
1. Eliminate immediate mate/tactical threats
2. Trade attacking pieces, not defenders
3. Build escape squares for king
4. Seek simplification into holdable endgame

The defender often survives by forcing checks, exchanges, and king activity.

## Resource Checklist
- Perpetual check motifs
- Fortress setups
- Opposite-color bishop simplifications
- Counter-threats against loose opponent king

## Practical Advice
When worse, avoid panic speed. Spend time on critical moments and force your opponent to show precise winning technique.
