A useful repertoire is not "most theoretical"—it is *repeatable, understandable, and practical* for your level.

## Repertoire Design Rules
- Choose one main system vs 1.e4 and 1.d4
- Add one backup line for surprise value
- Prefer structures you understand in middlegame

Openings should lead to positions where your plans are clear.

## Study Split (Weekly)
- 40% model games
- 30% tactical motifs from your openings
- 20% move-order traps and transpositions
- 10% engine verification

## Repertoire File Format
For each line store:
- Critical position (diagram/FEN)
- Main plan in one sentence
- Tactical alerts
- Endgame tendencies

This keeps your prep actionable under tournament pressure.
