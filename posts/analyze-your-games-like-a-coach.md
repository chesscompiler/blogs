Post-game analysis is where long-term strength is built. Engines are useful, but only after your own thinking.

## 3-Pass Review Method
1. **Human pass:** write thoughts, plans, emotions
2. **Critical moments pass:** identify 3 turning points
3. **Engine pass:** verify tactics and alternatives

## Mistake Taxonomy
Tag each error as:
- Calculation
- Strategy
- Time management
- Emotional decision
