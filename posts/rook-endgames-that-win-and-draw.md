Rook endgames occur constantly, so small technical improvements create huge rating gains.

## Essential Principles
- Activate rook first
- Rook behind passed pawn
- Cut off enemy king
- Create checking distance

White’s winning plan combines king activation + rook activity.

## Must-Know Theoretical Positions
- Lucena (building a bridge)
- Philidor (third-rank defense)
- Vancura (rook pawn defense)

## Study Routine
- 15 minutes: one theoretical setup daily
- 15 minutes: play versus engine from equal rook endgame
- 5 minutes: summarize one principle in your own words

Consistency here converts many "should-be draws" into wins and many "lost" positions into saves.
