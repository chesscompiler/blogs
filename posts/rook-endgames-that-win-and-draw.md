Rook endgames appear constantly because rooks survive exchanges and passed pawns need escorts. They are practical, tactical, and unforgiving. Knowing a few core positions changes results far more than memorizing dozens of rare ones badly.

## What This Chapter Teaches
- Understand the ideas behind the Philidor and Lucena positions rather than memorizing them blindly.
- Use active rooks, king cutoffs, and checking distance in real games.
- Know why rook activity is often worth a pawn.
- Recognize when to check from the side, from behind, or to build a bridge.

## Practical Framework
- In rook endings, activity usually matters more than pawn count.
- Keep the rook behind passed pawns whenever practical, yours or the opponent's.
- Cut the king off before trying to win a pawn.
- If you are defending, seek checking distance and counterplay instead of passive suffering.

*The defending rook controls the rank and denies the king's advance.*

The Philidor works because the defender understands the important zone. The rook holds the line first and only starts checking once the pawn advances and the enemy king loses cover.

## Model Position 2: The Lucena bridge

<chess-board fen="2K5/4P3/8/8/8/5r2/8/2k2R2 w - - 0 1" highlight="d4,d7,e7,c8" arrows="f1-f4,f4-d4,d4-d7"></chess-board>
*The attacking rook builds a shield so the king can step out safely.*

The Lucena is less about a trick and more about coordination. The king, rook, and passed pawn each have one precise job, and the win comes from executing them in the right order.

## Common Mistakes
- Playing passive rook moves just to hold material.
- Putting the rook in front of your own passed pawn without a concrete reason.
- Entering a rook ending thinking all rook endings are drawn.
- Ignoring king cutoffs and checking distance.

## Training Work
- Set up Philidor and Lucena positions until you can play both sides accurately.
- Review your rook endings and note whether your rook was active or passive.
- Study practical rook endings from strong players, not just theoretical diagrams.

## Why This Chapter Matters
Rook endings reward activity and exact method. Learn the core structures well and many practical endings stop being mysterious.
