Tactics are the grammar of chess. You don’t need to memorize thousands of positions—you need to internalize recurring motifs.

## The 7 Patterns That Win Real Games
- Fork
- Pin
- Skewer
- Discovered attack
- Removal of defender
- Deflection
- Back-rank mate

A classic back-rank idea appears when king luft is missing.

## Pattern Recognition Workflow
1. Scan king safety
2. Count defenders and attackers
3. Identify overloaded pieces
4. Ask: "What if this defender disappeared?"

## Tactical Habit Loop
- **Before your move:** Find opponent threats.
- **Before ending your turn:** Recheck loose pieces (both sides).
- **After each game:** Save one tactical miss in a notebook.

## Avoiding Fake Tactics
Many combinations fail because of one intermediate move. Always verify:
- Opponent checking resources
- Counter-captures with tempo
- Quiet defensive moves

Train patterns until they become automatic, then your calculation depth becomes useful instead of chaotic.
