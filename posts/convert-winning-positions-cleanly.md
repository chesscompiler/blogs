Many players earn winning positions and then let them slip. Conversion is a skill with clear rules.

## Conversion Framework
1. Remove counterplay
2. Improve king safety
3. Trade active opponent pieces
4. Keep enough material to finish

<chess-board fen="4k3/8/8/3P4/8/8/4K3/6R1 w - - 0 1" arrows="g1-g8,e2-e3,d5-d6"></chess-board>

White wins by coordinating rook + king while denying checks.

## Time-Management Rule
When clearly better, spend extra time once to choose a low-risk plan, then execute quickly. Avoid repeated re-evaluation every move.

## Psychological Rule
Do not hunt beauty. The best conversion move is often simple, technical, and slightly boring—and that is exactly why it works.
